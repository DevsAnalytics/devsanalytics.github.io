Ransomware Watch

Ransomware and B2B payment fraud are different crimes; however, the two connect when a vendor is compromised. Ransomware is an intrusion: attackers break into a company's systems, lock or steal its data, and demand money. Payment fraud is deception: someone poses as a vendor or executive to redirect a payment.

Below are organizations recently named on ransomware leak sites. This is not a list of fraud cases, and the listings are claims made by criminal groups which can be unverified or inaccurate.

The habit is the same either way: before paying new bank details, call a known contact at the vendor using a number you already have on file. Never use the number in the email.
